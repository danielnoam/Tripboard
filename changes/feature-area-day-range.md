### Changed
- Plan › Destination: "Set the days for all" takes one day or a range. One day moves everything in the destination to it; a range becomes the destination's dates, and what isn't on one of those days goes to its Any day.
- Plan › Destination on a computer: drop a destination on any empty space in the board to give it a block of its own, and the drop strip stays at the top of the view while you drag.
