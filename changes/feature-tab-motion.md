### Added
- Moving between a trip's tabs now slides: the new tab comes in from its side of the bar and settles with a small bounce, and the old one leaves the other way (mirrored in Hebrew). The circle in the bar slides to the new tab with a little stretch and bounce, and the icon swells, settles back and turns white. The circle never goes past the ends of the bar. With Reduced motion on in the device's settings the screens cross-fade quickly instead. Everything else in the app still doesn't animate.

- Today is now the middle tab: Plan, Map, Today, Expenses, Files.
- The + is now a small tab on top of the middle of the bar instead of a green circle at its end.
