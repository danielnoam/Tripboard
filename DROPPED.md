# Dropped

What was decided against, and why, so it doesn't come back. One entry
each: the idea, the date, and Daniel's reason.

- **Importing LifeLog's old trips** (`travel.json`), 2026-11-15: Daniel never really used LifeLog's Travel tab, so there's nothing worth bringing over.
- **Storage control** (what takes space, clearing it), 2026-11-15: Daniel took it off the list. Storage already shows the space used and asks the browser to keep the data.
- **Booking details on stays** (meals included, booking reference, booked with), 2026-11-23: Daniel saw the mockup and found it too much to see and keep up to date. A link to the booking, attached to the stay in Files, is enough. Only check-in and check-out were kept, as the stay's times.
- **Plan views by Category, Importance, Rating and Distance**, 2026-11-25: Daniel found them much less useful than Time and Destination, which answer when and where. They only re-sorted the same places. Category, importance and rating are filters now (rating: 5 stars, 4 or 3 and up, or not rated yet). Distance went too: the Map's locate shows what's near better than a list. What went with them: dragging a card between those groups (or Move in them) to change its category, importance or rating; each place is edited on its own now. The CLI's get_board takes only time and area, and find_places still lists by category, importance, rating or distance.
- **More from the weather** (rain warnings on outdoor places, what to pack, typical weather for trips more than 16 days off), 2026-11-25: Daniel took it off the list. The day-by-day forecast stays.
